From idea to drawings in three steps

You don't need CAD experience. If you can drag and drop, you can plan a house.

Pick or draw

Start from a ready-made design, or click a furnished room and drop it on the plan. Walls, door and fittings come with it.

Walk it and export

Walk through the house in 3D, then download a PDF set with plans, elevations, setting-out, schedules and quantities.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. There's no account, no sign-up and no payment. Open the app and start designing.

Where is my project saved?

It autosaves in your own browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded. Use Save to download a .json file you can back up, share, or open on another computer.

Can I build straight from the PDF?

The drawings are a strong starting point for your engineer and mason. The structural notes are typical practice only; a licensed structural engineer must check them before construction.

Which device should I use?

A laptop or desktop with a mouse works best. Use a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, and keep an internet connection for the 3D view and PDF export.
